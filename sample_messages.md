## 1. Board / Executive Team — business value and governance
**Subject: Demand Forecasting Pilot — Outcomes, Risks, and Review Cadence**

We are preparing a controlled demand forecasting pilot to improve visibility across sales, production, and procurement planning. During the pilot, forecast recommendations will be reviewed alongside business context rather than treated as automatic instructions. We will report forecast accuracy, service levels, inventory impact, user feedback, and material risks at the agreed review points. Decisions requiring changes to scope, investment, or risk tolerance will be escalated to the steering group. The next update will summarise pilot readiness, open risks, and decisions required.

## 2. Planners — partnership and retained human judgement
**Subject: Planner Forum: Test the Forecast, Share Exceptions**

The forecasting model is intended to support planning decisions, not remove planner expertise. Your knowledge of customer changes, production constraints, and unusual orders is essential to checking its recommendations. In the next planner forum, we will review example forecasts, identify where they differ from planner judgement, and record what needs to change. Please bring one example where a forecast seems unrealistic and explain the context. We will publish the actions, owners, and follow-up dates after the session.

## 4. Procurement Team — supply planning and uncertainty
**Subject: Forecast Pilot — Procurement Review and Supplier Coordination**

The pilot will provide a shared view of expected demand, but forecast values may change as new orders and market information arrive. Please review forecast recommendations alongside lead times, minimum order quantities, current stock, and supplier constraints. Flag any recommendation that could create excess stock, shortages, or an unrealistic order schedule. During the pilot, we will review material exceptions weekly and agree which changes require human approval before purchase orders are adjusted.

## 5. Key Yarn Supplier — collaboration and realistic commitments
**Subject: Improving Demand Visibility During the Forecasting Pilot**

We are testing a demand forecasting process to improve forward visibility of yarn requirements. The figures shared during the pilot should be treated as planning estimates, not firm purchase commitments, unless confirmed through the usual ordering process. We would appreciate your feedback on lead times, capacity constraints, minimum order quantities, and the notice period you need to respond reliably. We will agree a regular review cadence and a clear process for communicating significant changes, so neither party relies on an unconfirmed forecast as a final order.
